02

Protect your dashboard

Checking

In Zero Trust → Access controls → Applications, protect your production Pages URL and allow only your email. Then add these three Pages Settings → Variables and Secrets values:

CF_ACCESS_TEAM_DOMAIN—
CF_ACCESS_AUD—
ADMIN_EMAIL—
Open Cloudflare Zero Trust ↗
Where do I find these values?

Team domain: your yourteam.cloudflareaccess.com hostname (no https://). AUD: Access → Applications → your app → Configure → Additional settings → Application Audience (AUD) Tag. Admin email: the exact email allowed by the Access policy. Set these for Production and redeploy.

Cloudflare's Pages Access toggle may cover only preview deployments. Verify that your main *.pages.dev URL is protected too.
